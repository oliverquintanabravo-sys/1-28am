# Team Lead Toolkit — Overview

**Status:** shipped (v4); maintained
**Started:** [date? — v4 files dated 2026-06-28]

## What it is
An internal self-serve website for the Center's Expanded Learning Team Leads: "Everything from the workshop, in one place — the tools to run your classroom with confidence all year, not just on training day." 13 toolkit sections ("one for every real question… a new Team Lead actually asks on the floor"), the 5-day weekly framework, agency values, contact info, and ⌘K search. Positioning: "Built by a Team Lead, for the next one" — it supplements, never replaces, the Site Coordinator.

## Why it exists
The "From Intern to Team Lead" workshop ([[2026-07-08_intern-to-team-lead-workshop]]) happens once; the questions it answers come up all year. The Toolkit makes the answers reachable "in 30 seconds when you're standing in your classroom and need an answer."
