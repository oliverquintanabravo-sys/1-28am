# The "From Intern to Team Lead" workshop — Oliver's flagship training

Workshop by Oliver Quintana Bravo: "Owning Your Classroom, Building Your Impact." Ten-part roadmap: Icebreaker → My Journey → Why It Matters → Intern → Team Lead mindset shift → Paychex & Pro Basics → Work Plans ("plan a week that runs itself") → Enrichment ("activities students remember") → Structured Play ("where bonding happens") → Behavior & Safety ("lead calm, keep everyone safe") → The Year Ahead.

Signature moves worth reusing:
- Opens with a 3-minute icebreaker: name, site, favorite quote — "Leadership begins with knowing who you are. Your quote says something about how you'll lead."
- Teaches from his own trajectory: Intern → leading activities → Team Lead → State Assembly → CA Dept. of Education. "I didn't start as a confident Team Lead. I grew into it — one responsibility at a time."
- Uses a real filled-out week (Mr. Oliver · Sierra View) as the work-plan teaching example, not a blank form.
- Includes step-by-step Paychex Flex guides (dashboard, clock in/out, sick time, timecard approval) with "the circles show exactly where to tap."

The workshop's practical content became the Team Lead Toolkit website so it lives "all year, not just on training day" — see `projects/team_lead_toolkit/`.
