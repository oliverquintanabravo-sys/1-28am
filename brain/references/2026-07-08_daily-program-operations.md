# The Team Leader's daily operating routine and logistics

## Check-in (5 min)
1. Clock in on Paychex
2. Grab a walkie-talkie — know station number, emergency codes, charging/pickup location, proper communication protocol
3. Grab lanyard/ID badge — **visible at all times, stays on campus/site** (unless supporting Before School Program at another site)
4. Check daily announcements

## Prep time (40–45 min planning)
Load carts for the three blocks:
- **Academic support:** pencils, erasers, rulers, scissors, paper, coloring utensils
- **Enrichment:** materials in cart or classroom ready to go (construction paper, glue…)
- **Physical activity:** soccer balls, jump ropes, cones, hula hoops — **BASICS framework; line of sight is critical**

## Schedule mechanics
- Typical schedule includes **45 minutes of prep time**
- Early-release days: TRUSD shortened days, minimum days, **every Wednesday**
- **Program ends 6:00 pm** — late pickups have incident-reporting impact

## Homework support specifics
After-homework activities: worksheets, approved websites, school programs on laptops, books, materials for students

## Dress code & professionalism
- Casual wear, but observe the school's dress code; scent awareness — **arrive out of code and you'll be asked to change**
- Customer service to ALL stakeholders; over-communication; represent agency values

## Facilities rule: leave the space better than you found it
- Teacher collaboration; communicate missing/damaged teacher items via protocol
- **Program supplies only** (never teacher's classroom items)
- Clean-up routine after supper, after physical activity, after classroom use
