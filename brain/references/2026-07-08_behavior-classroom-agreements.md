# Behavior system: "Same House, Same Rule" and classroom agreements

## Site-level alignment
- Program-wide expectations = school alignment: **"Same House, Same Rule"**
- **Positive reinforcement only — NO yelling, threats, or negativity**
- District alignment varies by site: **PBIS**, **CHAMPS**

## Classroom agreements (the exact format)
- **3–5 agreements**, created **with student buy-in**, in the **first week** with the class
- Align with the day-teacher's agreements where possible
- Written in **grade-level language**
- Used as tools for redirection, intervention, and reinforcement of expectations

## Why it matters
This is the behavioral operating system for the classroom; any behavior-related plan, note, or training material must be consistent with it.
