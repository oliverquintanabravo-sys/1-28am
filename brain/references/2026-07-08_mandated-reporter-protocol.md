# Mandated reporter protocol — the exact 2-step process

## Who is a mandated reporter
People whose duties require direct contact and supervision of children — every Team Lead.

## When to report
On **knowledge or reasonable suspicion** of child abuse and/or neglect obtained in professional capacity or scope of employment. **It is NOT required to prove abuse/neglect to make a report.**

## How to file — the 2-step process
1. **Phone report: call (916) 875-KIDS**
2. **Written report within 24 hours** of the phone report — you receive a code to file **Justice form SS 8572**

## Operational notes (from the safety training)
Confidential · document everything · have the phone number accessible · you must be clocked in

## Why it matters
This is a legal duty with a hard 24-hour clock. It is the single most important protocol in the entire policy set — memorize the number and the two steps.
