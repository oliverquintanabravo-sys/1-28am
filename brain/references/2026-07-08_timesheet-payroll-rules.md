# Timesheet, payroll, and break rules (Paychex Flex)

## Account & timecard procedures
- Clock in and out **in real time**, per the confirmed weekly work schedule; keep Location on
- Corrections or edits: email the **Program Manager ASAP** — never self-fix silently
- Approve your own timecard each period (Paychex: review period → Approve All → "I Agree")
- Keep mailing address / personal info updated in Paychex; handbook lives on the Paychex account
- Payroll calendar comes from the site (aligned to the school calendar)

## Unpaid lunch break (30 min)
- **Required after 6 hours** of work; unpaid; signed waiver on file
- **Meal premium** owed for a missed break; non-compliance triggers employee and/or program assessment

## Rest periods (10 min)
- **Required after 3.5 hours** of work; **no clock-out required**
- Supervisor provides one unless the employee declines
- Approving the timecard = acknowledgement of rest periods
- No capacity to provide the break → complete a **"Notice of Missed Rest Period"** → **break premium (1 hour)**

## Why it matters
These are wage-law compliance items — the two premiums (meal, 1-hour break) are the expensive failure modes, and timecard approval legally acknowledges rest periods.
