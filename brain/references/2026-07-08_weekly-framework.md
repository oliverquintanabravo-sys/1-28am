# The weekly framework: five daily themes the program week is built on

Oliver's classroom runs on a fixed daily rhythm — "a simple theme for each day. Consistency is what makes behavior, planning, and engagement easier."

| Day | Theme |
|---|---|
| Monday | STEM |
| Tuesday | Arts & Creativity |
| Wednesday | Physical Activity |
| Thursday | Career Exploration |
| Friday | Community & Reflection |

## Why it matters
Every weekly work plan ([[2026-07-08_work-plan-format]]) hangs enrichment off these themes; the Toolkit teaches new Team Leads to build their week on it.
