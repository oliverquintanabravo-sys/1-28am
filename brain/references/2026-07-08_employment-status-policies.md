# Employment status, probation, and leave policies

## Status
- Team Leader and Senior Team Leader are **part-time casual** roles
- Employment **ends at the end of the academic year**; return via **"Intent to Return"** submission in good standing (TRUSD staff waitlist: trusd.admin@sccsc.org)

## 90-day probationary period
- At-will separation during probation; named causes: **insubordination, student safety, attendance**

## Leaves of absence
- Requested with validated reasoning; approved by the **Executive Director** (except Family Leave), via email to direct supervisor
- **Personal:** extended unpaid time off with attendance accountability and a return date
- **Medical:** may use sick-time benefit and other PTO
- **Family:** state/federal benefit (maternity/paternity); employee coordinates with **EDD**, communicates estimated leave/return to agency
- **No PTO accrual or holiday pay during leave**; extensions past the return date require a new email request; the Center holds the position for returning employees

## Handbook policies to know (on Paychex)
Conduct in the workplace · dress code · cell phone usage · tobacco- and drug-free workplace · probationary period · leave of absence · no-call-no-show
