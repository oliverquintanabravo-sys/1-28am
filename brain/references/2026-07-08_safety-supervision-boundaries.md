# Safety, supervision, and student-boundary rules

## Emergency preparedness
- Drills — **fire, earthquake, intruder — three times a year**
- Know the emergency school map: exit routes, safety-zone location, **red/green cards**
- Know first-aid supply locations; give your emergency contact info to the Program Manager

## Campus security
- **No one on campus without a visitor or district badge** — radio the Program Manager; only tell the person to leave yourself if you feel comfortable
- **Authorized pickup only via Procare or 6Crickets**; PM notifies of changes
- Cameras are on campus — assume everything is observed
- Incident reports: documentation, always

## Supervision best practices
- **Constant line-of-sight supervision: watch, count, and listen**; clear sightlines, easy access to children
- Active and engaged supervision — anticipate what children may do, redirect when necessary
- **Whole-group restroom breaks**
- Attention getters: verbal and non-verbal

## Student boundaries (hard rules)
- Mindful of physical touch (hugging etc.); careful when speaking about students to others
- **Never**: add students on social media · share personal phone/email · share items or food with students · let students access staff bags/personal belongings
- No student access to iPads or Procare; **student insight is CONFIDENTIAL**
- Redirect inappropriate language via program guidelines (communicate with PM)

## During program — know at all times
Class and supper roster · student allergies, medication, walker status, photo release · program schedule · daily checklist · behavior guidelines · emergency procedures
