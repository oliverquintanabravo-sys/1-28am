# The 6 Quality Standards every work-plan day is marked against

"Mark these on every day of your plan — they keep activities intentional."

1. Safe & Supportive Environment
2. Active & Engaged Learning
3. Skill Building
4. Youth Voice & Leadership
5. Healthy Choices & Behaviors
6. Diversity, Access & Equity
6a. Supporting English Learners

## Why it matters
These are the checkboxes (1–6a) on the official Weekly Team Leader Work Plan form; any AI-drafted plan must state which standards each day hits.
