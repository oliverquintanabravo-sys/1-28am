# The weekly work plan format, from Oliver's filled example

Official form: "Weekly Team Leader Work Plans" (theCenter). Header: School Site / Team Leader / "Week of ____". One section per program day, each containing:

1. **Initiative** — the daily theme (e.g., "Monday Initiative: STEM")
2. **Quality Standards** — checkboxes 1–6a ([[2026-07-08_six-quality-standards]])
3. **Academic Support & Literacy** — Oliver's pattern: whole-group homework review at the board → 1-on-1 help → early finishers on i-Ready / Reflex Math (Chromebooks) or independent reading
4. **Structured Physical Activity** — one structured game + a cool-down (stretching circle, deep breaths)
5. **Enrichment** — objective/description including the *key teaching moment*, e.g. Paper Cup Estimation Challenge: 3 rounds of guessing (looking → poured out → handful); teaching moment is whether students revise answers — "hypothesis, test, observe, revise"
6. **Materials** — complete prep list (paper cups 1/student, scrap paper, pencils, whiteboard, Chromebooks…)

Ongoing-program example: the Career Exploration Program — each student writes their dream career on a card; one card drawn per week becomes the enrichment theme until every name is called. "Every student gets their moment."
