# Attendance, absence, and communication policies

## Unplanned absence
- Notify **24 hours in advance or ASAP** via call, text, or email, with the reason
- **3rd consecutive illness absence** → physician's note required clearing safe return

## Tardiness
- Tell the Program Manager by phone/email/text **at least 3 hours in advance** (or ASAP) with reason + ETA

## Planned time off
- Submit the time-off request **form** in advance; approval or denial (with reason) arrives in writing

## Sick leave (part-time casual employees)
- Accrue **1 hour per 30 hours worked**, capped at **40 hours per calendar year**

## No call / no show
- **Job abandonment after the 3rd consecutive day**; administration reaches out via email AND phone

## Contact routing
- Hiring & resignation protocols: **careers@sccsc.org** (slide 25) — but slide 37 says resignation MUST go to **trusd.admin@sccsc.org / nusd.admin@sccsc.org**. ⚠️ Contradiction between slides; confirm the correct channel before ever acting on it.
- All other employee inquiries: trusd.admin@sccsc.org or nusd.admin@sccsc.org
- Employees must actively monitor email, text/phone, and Paychex messages during work hours

## Staff meetings
- **Once a month, 1 hour**, scheduled before or after the work shift (in addition to the 45-min prep where applicable); announced via calendar, email, and staff boards
