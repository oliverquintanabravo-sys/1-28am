# Employee performance assessment structure

## Observations
- **Informal:** frequent check-ins to coach and support
- **Formal:** district and/or agency-led observation, integrated with program-quality observations

## Assessments
- Documented through observation forms, supervisor emails, stakeholder feedback, and agency-admin observations

## Enhancements
- General performance-based, **aligned with funding capacity**

## Forms
- A **Corrective Action Plan** form exists for performance issues

## Why it matters
Work plans, quality-standards marking, and program observations feed directly into formal assessment — the weekly work plan isn't paperwork, it's evaluation evidence.
