## Process
1. **Load the daily themes:** Mon STEM · Tue Arts & Creativity · Wed Physical Activity · Thu Career Exploration · Fri Community & Reflection.
2. **Academic support block (per day):** whole-group homework review at the board → 1-on-1 help for students needing extra support → early-finisher option (i-Ready, Reflex Math on Chromebooks, or independent reading).
3. **Structured physical activity (per day):** one structured game matched to the day's energy (e.g., Duck Duck Goose for community-building, Would You Rather Showdown for movement + discussion) plus a cool-down (stretching circle, deep breaths).
4. **Enrichment (per day, the judgment step):** design or pick an activity that fits the daily theme, names its objective and the key teaching moment, and continues any ongoing program (Career Exploration rotation: draw one student's career card per week until every name is called).
5. **Materials list (per day):** everything needed, specific and complete (cups, scrap paper, pencils, whiteboard, Chromebooks…).
6. **Quality-standards check:** mark which of the 6 Quality Standards each day hits — they keep activities intentional: 1 Safe & Supportive Environment · 2 Active & Engaged Learning · 3 Skill Building · 4 Youth Voice & Leadership · 5 Healthy Choices & Behaviors · 6 Diversity, Access & Equity · 6a Supporting English Learners. See [[2026-07-08_six-quality-standards]].

## Quality gates
- [ ] Every program day has all three blocks filled
- [ ] Each enrichment has an objective AND a key teaching moment, not just an activity name
- [ ] Ongoing programs (Career Exploration) advanced, not forgotten
- [ ] Materials lists complete enough to prep from directly
- [ ] Activities age-appropriate for elementary and feasible in the time block
