**Status: v2 draft** — rebuilt 2026-07-08 from Oliver's filled plan + the official blank template + Fall Launch work-plan policy (slides 40–41). Awaiting one live run with Oliver; encode his corrections here.

## What this workflow is
Produces Oliver's weekly Team Leader Work Plan (Sierra View, the Center's Expanded Learning program): one section per program day — Initiative, Quality Standards, Materials, Academic Support & Literacy, Structured Physical Activity, Enrichment with Objective/Description. Plans are required for grants and the district, are used by a substitute when Oliver is absent, and feed formal performance assessment — write every day so a stranger could run it.

## Process
1. **Frame the week.** Fill header (School Site: Sierra View / Team Leader / Dates). Apply the daily themes: Mon STEM · Tue Arts & Creativity · Wed Physical Activity · Thu Career Exploration · Fri Community & Reflection. Note Wednesday is a TRUSD minimum day (program 12:00–6:00 pm) — Wednesday plans need more program hours covered.
2. **Carry forward last week's reflection.** Adjust this week's enrichment toward what students showed interest in — reflection is required policy, not optional polish.
3. **Academic Support & Literacy (per day).** Oliver's pattern: whole-group homework review at the board → 1-on-1 help → early finishers on i-Ready / Reflex Math (Chromebooks) or independent reading. Vary the early-finisher option across the week.
4. **Structured Physical Activity (per day).** One structured game matched to the day's energy + a cool-down (stretching circle, deep breaths). Must be runnable with line-of-sight supervision; note sports equipment needed (cart: balls, jump ropes, cones, hula hoops).
5. **Enrichment (per day — the judgment step).** Each activity needs: an **Objective/Description**, the **key teaching moment** (e.g., Paper Cup Estimation: do students revise answers when new evidence arrives — hypothesis, test, observe, revise), a tie to the daily theme, and continuation of ongoing programs (Career Exploration: draw the next career card; every student gets their moment before repeats).
6. **Plan B every day.** Policy requires inclusivity and Plan B's: an indoor alternative for outdoor activities and a low-materials fallback for enrichment.
7. **Materials (per day).** Complete list, prep-ready (quantities where relevant: "paper cups — 1 per student").
8. **Mark Quality Standards (per day).** Check which of 1–6a each day genuinely hits: 1 Safe & Supportive · 2 Active & Engaged · 3 Skill Building · 4 Youth Voice & Leadership · 5 Healthy Choices & Behaviors · 6 Diversity, Access & Equity · 6a Supporting English Learners. During the August–November window, ensure 1 and 2 are demonstrably present daily. Never check a standard the activity doesn't actually address.
9. **Render and deliver.** Format to the official layout (future: `execution/render_work_plan.py` — see `execution/designs/render_work_plan.md`). Deliver by the due date: upload to the drive or email the Program Manager.

## Quality gates
- [ ] All five program days complete — a substitute could run any day from the page alone
- [ ] Every enrichment has an objective AND a key teaching moment, not just an activity name
- [ ] Ongoing programs advanced (Career Exploration rotation not forgotten)
- [ ] Every day has a Plan B (weather / space / materials)
- [ ] Quality Standards checked honestly per day; 1 & 2 present daily during Aug–Nov focus
- [ ] Materials lists complete enough to load the cart without re-reading activity text
- [ ] Age-appropriate for elementary; fits the actual time blocks; Wednesday's longer hours covered
- [ ] No invented constraints, events, or student facts — placeholders + a question instead

## Edge cases
- Rainy/heat day → run the Plan B indoor structured game; note the swap on the plan.
- Wednesday (TRUSD minimum day) → program runs 12:00–6:00 pm; plan additional activity blocks.
- Oliver absent → the plan IS the substitute's script; write names of tools/locations explicitly (cart contents, walkie station, roster location).
- Assembly/district event eats a block → compress academic support before dropping enrichment; never drop physical activity entirely (program goal: lowering obesity rate).
