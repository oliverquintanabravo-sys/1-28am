## Observed style (AI-drafted from Oliver's Team Lead Toolkit copy, 2026-07-08 — Oliver: correct this)

- **Plain, warm, and practical.** Speaks directly to the reader's real situation: "the tools to run your classroom with confidence all year, not just on training day."
- **Peer-to-peer, not top-down:** "Built by a Team Lead, for the next one."
- **Anchored in real questions:** "one for every real question… something a new Team Lead actually asks on the floor."
- **Respects the reader's time:** content designed to be reached "in 30 seconds when you're standing in your classroom and need an answer."
- **Concrete instructions with visual anchors:** "The circles show exactly where to tap — phone and computer."
